Privacy Policy

QuickList is designed to keep task management simple, organized, and private. This policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Personal Information and Task Data

QuickList does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to manage their tasks.

Lists, tasks, and completion statuses are stored locally on the user's device.

Your tasks are not uploaded to a cloud account or synchronized with the developer's servers.

2. Software Updates and Technical Information

QuickList uses Expo's software-update services to check for and deliver compatible application updates.

During update requests, the service may process technical information, including:

  • A randomly generated application installation identifier.
  • The device's IP address.
  • Operating system and application version information.
  • Application update identifiers.
  • Diagnostic information associated with failed updates or application launch errors.

This information is processed to deliver updates, monitor update performance, and diagnose update-related problems.

QuickList does not use this information for advertising or tracking users across other companies' applications.

3. Third-Party Services

QuickList uses Expo's update infrastructure as a technical service provider.

You can review Expo's privacy practices at expo.dev/privacy .

QuickList does not include advertising and does not sell users' personal information.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

User-created lists and tasks remain on the device until they are deleted within QuickList or the application's local data is removed.

Uninstalling QuickList normally removes its locally stored application data.

Technical information processed by Expo is retained according to its applicable data-handling and retention practices.

Users may contact the developer with questions about information processed in connection with QuickList.

5. Data Security

QuickList stores task information locally on the user's device.

Communications with the software-update service use encrypted HTTPS connections.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

QuickList is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed for children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to QuickList's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements.

Updates will be reflected by a revised effective date on this page.
